# Absent missing

A value appears where the reference version has a missing value: missings are filled in, for instance by imputation.

## Pipeline implication examples

Cleaning or imputation steps may become unnecessary or redundant, and the imputed values may alter the distribution of the affected features. Features that encode missingness may lose their meaning.

## Typical adaptation

Review data validation and cleaning steps (imputation, missing-value indicators).
